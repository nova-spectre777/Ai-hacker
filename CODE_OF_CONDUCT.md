# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in Code Integrity Research Lab should be respectful, constructive and centered on technical evidence. We welcome good-faith questions, beginner contributions and disagreement about implementation choices.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and use inclusive, professional language.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Explain technical feedback with evidence where practical.
- Respect privacy and protect credentials, unpublished vulnerabilities and personal information.
- Follow the project's authorization and responsible-disclosure boundaries.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, discriminatory conduct, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, or encouraging unauthorized harm.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and may restrict participation when conduct violates this policy. Reports should be sent privately to the repository maintainer through GitHub. Handle reports discreetly and share details only with people who need them to respond.
